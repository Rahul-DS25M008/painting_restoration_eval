Limitations

  • The evidence evaluates digital reconstruction, not physical conservation treatment.
  • Repeated candidates and prompt variants are nested within cases and paintings.
  • SDXL has partial coverage and must not be compared as though it covered all cases.
  • Perceptual and feature models are not conservation-specific.
  • A better metric result does not establish historical authenticity.
  • A worse flag profile does not by itself establish unusable visual quality.
  • Overall model selection remains inconclusive when metric families disagree.
