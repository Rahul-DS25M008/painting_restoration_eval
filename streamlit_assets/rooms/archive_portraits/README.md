# Research Archive decorative portraits

Created for the Research Archive shelf frames using the built-in image-generation tool. The room shell and reference lettering were not modified. Images are decorative, non-interactive overlays; Leto II uses multiply blending to preserve the parchment through its white negative space.

- `azhdaha.png`: unofficial Genshin Impact fan artwork, based on the Achievement: Stone Dragon icon. Reference: https://game8.co/games/Genshin-Impact/archives/355558 (icon: https://img.game8.co/3448667/167b3e1526dd54e8b9358399acd4ba91.png/show).
- `leto_ii.png`: unofficial Dune fan artwork showing Leto II's human–sandworm form.
- `dragon.png`: original fantasy-dragon silhouette.

Third-party characters and franchise elements are excluded from the project's MIT/CC BY grants. This addition does not claim permission or blanket rights clearance. These are post-publication dashboard decorations, not changes to the frozen v1.0.0 Zenodo archive or experimental evidence.

## Final prompts

### Azhdaha

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a small decorative portrait silhouette to sit on existing brown parchment inside a museum bookshelf frame. Input image is SUBJECT REFERENCE ONLY: Azhdaha from Genshin Impact's Achievement: Stone Dragon icon. Create a single bold sepia-brown silhouette of this same distinctive horned dragon head and neck in side profile facing right: broad blunt stone-dragon muzzle, layered jaw, sweeping antler-like horns and crown. Preserve the recognizable head silhouette of the reference, simplify internal details to a few transparent carved lines. Dark warm umber ink around #65401f. Transparent background, not parchment. Centered portrait composition with 8% margin, entire horns and neck inside canvas. No frame, no square card backing, no scene, no text, no glow. It must read clearly when reduced to 30 pixels tall.

### Leto II

A black-and-white linocut illustration, white paper background. Leto II Atreides from God Emperor of Dune in his human-sandworm hybrid form. One majestic upright thick segmented worm body with a small bald HUMAN FACE embedded at the upper front, small human arms, broad curved tail at bottom, no legs. Strong solid dark sepia-brown silhouette with WIDE WHITE carved lines defining each body segment and facial features. Clearly show HUMAN eyes, nose, and mouth within the worm's upper hood. NO blur, NO shading, NO halo, NO photorealism. Crisp woodcut stamp emblem, very simple and bold. Vertical 2:3 composition, single entire figure with 8 percent white margin, no border, no letters, no environment. White background must be pure uniform white.

### Dragon

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: miniature decorative sepia silhouette inside a museum bookshelf picture frame. A striking original fantasy dragon in profile, a horned head on a graceful arched neck, one raised batlike wing, curled tail, perched compactly. Bold elegant silhouette like an antique brown ink engraving, a few transparent incised details only. Dark warm umber #65401f. True transparent background. Centered upright portrait composition with 8% padding and all horns, wings and tail contained. Strong silhouette readable at 30 pixels tall. No frame, no parchment backing, no text, no scene, no glow.
